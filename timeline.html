Chronology

The timeline

Ordered by where each chapter begins, not by the date of its last judgment. A sentence handed down in 2013 can belong to a case that started in 2010.

1982

01 · The Airport Job

1982—1983 — A medical detailer lands at Christchurch Airport charged with importing LSD and methamphetamine. Six months later a jury clears her on both counts.

1990

02 · Norwich Street

1990 — The archive’s first laboratory. Three men admit attempted manufacture and are remanded for sentence — the record of what followed has not been found.

2004

03 · Diablo

2004—2009 — A manufacture and supply network running Christchurch to Dunedin ends in the High Court. Five convictions, and individual terms that surface years later in fragments.

2007

04 · Tsunami

2007 — One conspiracy charge, seven defendants, two acquittals. An eight-year term is later upheld on appeal.

2010

05 · Granite

2010—2013 — Police announce three clandestine laboratories and a Hornby raid. The cases split: conspiracy acquittals, a conviction entered late, and terms spread across two years.

2011

06 · Larch Place

2011 — One address, three defendants, three different endings: a prison term, a withdrawal, and a charge the public record never resolves.

2019

07 · Maddale

2019—2023 — An Auckland arm, a Christchurch arm and cash moved through Avonside. Three terms are reported, two of them under appeal when the reporting stops.

2019

08 · The 2020 Ring

2019—2020 — A meth ring, four recorded terms, and a sentencing that waits on six months of rehabilitation.

2023

09 · Italian Sky

2023 — Eleven sentences from two linked national supply lines, reported together — from ten months’ home detention to nine years and three months.

2024

10 · Avon

2024—2026 — The largest cluster in this record: equipment charges, guilty pleas, a judge-alone trial and a mixed verdict that acquitted a commander on twenty-five charges.

2024

11 · Whoosh

2024—2026 — A darknet storefront, dozens of confirmed imports and a twelve-year sentence. Two courier terms depend on which report you read.

2010

12 · The Streets

2010—2025 — Bower Avenue, Redwood, Waltham, Hillsborough, Wordsworth Street. Charges and remands — and in most of them the public record stops before a finding.

P CITY

A sourced dossier of Christchurch methamphetamine laboratories, supply prosecutions and court outcomes, 1982—2026. Charges are not convictions; acquittals are not guilt. Presentation is inspired by the true-crime documentary genre and is not affiliated with, or derived from, any television production.
