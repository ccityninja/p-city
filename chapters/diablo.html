TWO CITIES

Diablo

2004—2009 · 6 case records · 3 sourced terms

Five guilty verdicts in the High Court at Christchurch in June 2004, and a network that ran between Christchurch and Dunedin. The individual sentences surfaced years apart: five years for Samson in a later property report, two years for Beattie, twenty-one months for the courier Musselwhite. For Tate, Rymer and Carruth the term is still not in the record. 18234445

3 sentenced3 unresolved
Record note — what is settled in this file and what is not

The verdict report identifies Samson, Tate, Rymer, Beattie and Carruth. A later property report gives Samson five years; a separate retrospective gives Beattie two years, and a court follow-up gives associated courier Musselwhite 21 months. The latter was not one of the five verdict-day defendants. Tate, Rymer and Carruth’s individual terms remain unknown. Beattie’s separate four-and-a-half-year term must not be substituted for his Diablo sentence. 18234445
