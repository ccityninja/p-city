Record note — what is settled in this file and what is not

The later court report expressly links Christchurch resident Charlie Tuatini Tereora’s six-year sentence to Maddale. It describes him as the link between the Auckland and Christchurch arms and the cash custodian. Safi’s eleven years and Esefehani-Bahadori’s five-and-a-half years are original terms under appeal; the retrieved hearing report reserves the decision. An unnamed car importer received a four-year effective concurrent sentence, not seven years. Earlier and later arrest totals are dated reports, not a single fixed census. 22283435
