The Airport Job

1982—1983 · 1 case records · 0 sourced terms

A charge of importing LSD and methamphetamine at Christchurch Airport, laid in September 1982, is the earliest city-linked prosecution in this record. In March 1983 a jury found Joanne Forrest-Smith not guilty on both counts and she was discharged. An acquittal, not a laboratory find — and not proof that a laboratory existed. 6061

Record note — what is settled in this file and what is not

The contemporaneous newspaper reports connect Joanne Forrest-Smith to alleged importation at Christchurch Airport in September 1982, then report her acquittal and discharge in March 1983. This is the earliest such prosecution located in the searched material; it does not establish a successful importation or the first methamphetamine ever present in the city. The original lab archive begins with the January 1990 Norwich Street discovery. 6061
