THE FIRST LAB

Norwich Street

1990 · 3 case records · 0 sourced terms

In January 1990 a methamphetamine laboratory was found at a Norwich Street address — the first entry in this archive. Three men pleaded guilty to attempted manufacture and to possessing the instruments, and were remanded on bail for sentencing on 13 July 1990. What the court imposed that day is not in the material recovered for this record. 3
