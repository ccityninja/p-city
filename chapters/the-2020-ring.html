The 2020 Ring

2019—2020 · 5 case records · 4 sourced terms

Five men, one methamphetamine ring, February 2020. Smith got five years nine months plus three more for a prison-yard fight on remand; Powhiro six years three months; Ross two years nine months; Fahey two years two months. Howard pleaded guilty and his sentencing was postponed for a six-month rehabilitation programme. The record ends there. 19

Record note — what is settled in this file and what is not

The report establishes Andrew Michael Smith’s five-year-nine-month drug term plus three months for a remand fight, Ross’s two years nine months, Powhiro’s six years three months and Fahey’s earlier two years two months. Howard’s sentencing was postponed for rehabilitation; no subsequent outcome was located. The same full name in Italian Sky is not enough to merge identities. 19
