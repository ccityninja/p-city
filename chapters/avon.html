COMANCHEROS

Avon

2024—2026 · 19 case records · 16 sourced terms

Nineteen case records from the Comancheros investigation: equipment charges, guilty pleas and a judge-alone trial. Larenz Smith drew ten years, Bolstad eight years eight months, Sua eight years three months. In July 2026 a commander was acquitted on twenty-five charges and sentenced to thirteen months on another. For Singer and Dodd the record holds a hearing, not a sentence. 1112152024293031

Record note — what is settled in this file and what is not

The missing Higgins, young Wikaira, secretary Wikaira and Olliver sentences are now recovered. Olliver’s father–son relationship with Larenz Smith is explicit in the court report. Naufahu’s acquittals on 25 serious charges are kept separate from his later personal cocaine conviction and 13-month sentence. Dodd’s guilty verdict and adjourned sentencing are now explicit; Singer’s scheduled June hearing is not proof of a sentence. 1112152024293031
