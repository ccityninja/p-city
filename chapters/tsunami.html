SEVEN ACCUSED

Tsunami

2007 · 8 case records · 2 sourced terms

One conspiracy charge, seven defendants, March 2007. Five were found guilty; Teresa Boyce and Kexi Chen were acquitted and discharged. Deborah Gordon-Smith’s eight years — six for the methamphetamine conspiracy plus two cumulative for ecstasy supply — survived a sentence appeal. Rong-Jun Sui’s nine years four months came after an admission. The other individual terms were never recovered. 4647

Record note — what is settled in this file and what is not

The verdict report is dated March 2007, not an unknown trial year. Five named defendants received guilty verdicts; Boyce and Chen were acquitted. Primary appeal text establishes Gordon-Smith’s eight-year effective sentence and dismissal of her sentence appeal. The source also reports a prior nine-year-four-month term for Rong-Jun Sui. The other four defendants’ terms remain unrecovered. 4647
