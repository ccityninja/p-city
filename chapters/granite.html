THREE LABS

Granite

2010—2013 · 9 case records · 8 sourced terms

The June 2010 police release announced three clandestine laboratories. What followed took years to settle: Michael Leslie Smith and Adam Miles were acquitted of conspiracy but sentenced on other counts, John Douglas McKenzie’s conviction was entered late and sentenced in December 2013, and Matthew Newton was reported guilty on every charge with no term recovered. Eight terms are in this record. 17373839404142434849

6 sentenced2 split verdict1 unresolved
Record note — what is settled in this file and what is not

The June 2010 Police release, contemporary court report and later judgments corroborate one investigation. Recovered judgments now establish individual sentences while preserving Smith’s and Miles’s conspiracy acquittals. McKenzie’s initially unentered conviction was later entered and he was eventually sentenced. Newton’s final term remains unrecovered; starting points are not end sentences. 17373839404142434849
