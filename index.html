Read this first

This is a court record dressed as a case file, not a drama. Every number and quotation on these pages traces to a cited source; nine terms are checked against the primary judgments themselves. Where a person was acquitted, that acquittal is printed. Where a sentence was never recovered, the page says so.

78 case records are not 78 unique people. Two entries share the name Andrew Michael Smith without a proven cross-case match, and one disputed pair of courier terms is flagged rather than resolved by preference.
