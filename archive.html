Norwich Street — earliest Christchurch case recovered

1990-01 → 1990-07-02 · Christchurch urban area
Guilty pleas to attempted manufacture and equipment possession; final sentences unlocated

The Press of 20 January names the three defendants on drug charges. Its 3 July report identifies a January search of Wayne Anthony Spackman’s Norwich Street home and a laboratory in the laundry. Richard Grant Cavaney, Spackman and Peter Wallace Marriott pleaded guilty on 2 July after the Crown amended manufacture to attempted manufacture; they also admitted the equipment charge. Sentencing was scheduled for 13 July. This is the earliest Christchurch case currently documented in this collection, not proof that no earlier laboratory existed.
