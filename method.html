Method & limits

How this was built

Source-backed Christchurch meth-laboratory and linked supply-case research through 9 November 2026. Retrieved primary judgments are labelled separately from media reports and Police allegations. Source-supported means checked against the retrieved text, not that every media account was independently verified in court. The supplement counts case-person rows, not unique human identities; one published name appears in separate cases without a proven identity match. National and wider-Canterbury networks are linked context, not additional city laboratory finds. Disputed sentence terms are excluded from supported-term totals. Absence from searched public sources does not establish absence of a court outcome.

The rules

  • A charge is not a conviction. A guilty plea is not a sentence. A scheduled hearing is not an outcome.
  • An acquittal is printed wherever it exists, including inside a mixed verdict.
  • A missing outcome is recorded as missing. It is never inferred from silence, and silence is not treated as innocence or guilt.
  • Every number and distinctive phrase in the written narratives was matched verbatim against the cited source file before publication. Nine terms are checked against the primary judgment text itself.
  • Where reports conflict — as they do for two Whoosh courier terms — both are shown and neither is preferred.

Identity traps

Shared names are the most dangerous part of a record like this. The register keeps these apart deliberately, and drops entries that could not be sourced rather than patching over them.
